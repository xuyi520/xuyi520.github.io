**Xu Yi (Ian, 许逸)**  
M.A. in Applied Psychology, Beijing Normal University (2025), *Outstanding Graduate Student*.

I build **computational models of self-control and cognition**. My recent work reframes the ego-depletion debate with a **coupled dynamical-systems** account: self-control emerges from the interaction of **depletion** and **activation** processes. This helps explain the high variability and **replication difficulties** that linear main-effect designs struggle with.

From **September 2025**, I will join the **Joint AI & Psychology Laboratory** (CUHK-Shenzhen × USTC) as a full-time research assistant under **Dr. Zhicheng Lin**, focusing on **AI × cognitive science**:
- Using AI and computational modeling to study cognitive/psychological mechanisms.
- Using cognitive principles to inspire **more interpretable, human-like AI**.

## Research Interests

- **Computational Modeling of Cognition** — formal models explaining complex cognitive processes.
- **Cognitive Science × Artificial Intelligence** — bidirectional integration of methods and insights.
- **AI and Human Behavior** — how behavior shapes and is shaped by intelligent systems.
- **Metascience** — reliability, heterogeneity, and research practices.
